Common questions

Do I need to bring my own clubs?

Nope — we have loaner clubs available if you need them. Of course you're welcome to bring your own set.

How many people fit in a simulator bay?

Each bay comfortably fits up to 4 players. For larger groups, check out our private event bookings.

Are the lessons good for complete beginners?

Absolutely. Our pro works with all skill levels — from never-held-a-club to competitive golfers wanting to improve.

Can I walk in or do I need a reservation?

Walk-ins are welcome when bays are open, but we recommend booking online to guarantee your preferred time.
